Q1. Employee Bonus System 🔥🔥

Given:
employees = [
    ("Harsh", 45000, 5),
    ("Rahul", 28000, 2),
    ("Amit", 55000, 6),
    ("Neha", 32000, 3),
    ("Priya", 70000, 8)
]

Each tuple contains:

(name, salary, years_of_experience)

Create a function calculate_bonus(employees).
Rules:

Only employees with 3 or more years of experience are eligible.
Eligible employees receive a 10% salary bonus.
Use filter() to select eligible employees.
Use map() to calculate the bonus salary.
Return:
[49500, 60500, 35200, 77000]

Original numbers
